Frequently Asked Questions

Is dental treatment painful?

Modern dentistry is largely pain-free. We use advanced local anesthetics and minimally invasive techniques to ensure your complete comfort during any procedure.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a comprehensive check-up and professional cleaning every six months to maintain optimal oral health and catch potential issues early.

How long does teeth whitening take?

Our in-clinic professional laser whitening typically takes about 60 to 90 minutes and can brighten your teeth by several shades in a single visit.
